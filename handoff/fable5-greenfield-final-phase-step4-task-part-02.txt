- Are rollback claims stronger than the substrate contract supports?

F. Handoff
- Can the no-re-asking rule preserve a stale or contextually invalid answer?
- Can the one-safe-next-action rule become an oversimplification?
- Can package tiers and trigger slots remain indefinitely unresolved?
- Does recovery depend on too many source artifacts?

G. Validation and self-evaluation
- Is the architecture evaluating itself with its own assumptions?
- Are no-write proofs, check splits, or PASS non-escalation practically
  enforceable?
- Can the evaluation package become another unverified truth source?
- Is the proposed validation path falsifiable?

H. Privacy and originals
- Does external custody merely move risk elsewhere?
- Are pointer rot, deletion, correction, withdrawal, disclosure, and history
  exposure adequately bounded?
- Do conservative defaults pressure the user into policy decisions?

I. Automation and surfaces
- Is AUTO staging coherent across all six profiles?
- Does AUTO4 or adapter behavior depend on dated facts?
- Are manual/plain-dialogue paths genuinely usable, or nominal fallbacks?
- Are local and hosted profiles overgeneralized?

J. Multi-project and global lessons
- Can namespace metadata or lineage checks prevent semantic contamination?
- Can project lessons be incorrectly generalized despite formal promotion?
- Are global-rule rollback and project pinning coherent under change?

K. Migration and portability
- Are export fidelity, adapter replacement, and rollback assumed rather than
  demonstrated?
- What happens when the destination cannot represent a core semantic artifact?
- Are hidden or platform-owned state losses sufficiently handled?

L. Cost and maintainability
- What human, computational, storage, and coordination costs dominate?
- Which mechanisms create the most operational friction?
- Where might users bypass governance because it is too heavy?
- Which parts are likely to decay without automation?
