## 12. Gate J — Evidence calibration

The report generally labels direct, analogical, systematic-review, conceptual and engineering evidence appropriately. The following corrections are required:

1. The **integrated local closed-loop policy** is an engineering synthesis supported by multiple traditions; its maturity must not be labeled `replicated_peer_reviewed`.
2. The claim that a broad self-description is a weak prior is reasonable, but concept-inventory predictive-validity studies do not directly compare broad self-report with local diagnostic evidence.
3. Ordinary dialogue’s ability to distinguish retrieval failure, connection gaps, notation barriers, task misunderstanding or tutor misunderstanding is incompletely validated. Treat these as testable hypotheses.
4. Physics, K–12 mathematics, science education, programming, health teach-back and commercial ITS results are adjacent or analogical when transferred to university foundational mathematics dialogue.
5. Numerical confidence values are absent; the qualitative `moderate` final confidence is appropriate.
