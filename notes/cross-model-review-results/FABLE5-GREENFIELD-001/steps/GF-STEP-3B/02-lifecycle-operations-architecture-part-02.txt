## 10. Roles and responsibilities

**H — human owner.** Sole authority source: every AC6 act, custody exceptions, retention classes, adoption of any MC/PC instrument, automation-stage enablement, migration continuation, promotion activation. Never delegable. **M — active model/agent.** Drafts AC3/AC4/AC5/AC8/AC9/AC12 and restatements; proposes; executes only within the active automation stage and surface profile; its claims are never evidence (B11) and never create transitions. **T — deterministic tooling.** Hashing and digest verification, schema/link/pin checks, single-current invariant, journal append mechanics, staleness sweeps, no-write proofs, index lookups; exercises no judgment; carries no authority. **S — repository/storage substrate.** Provides A16 contract semantics only (addressing, history, append-only zones, access classes, export, audit evidence); enforces nothing semantic. **RV — optional model-assisted reviewer.** Advisory critique of candidates, packages, and validation objects; never sole judge (LD-08, UA-14); its output is R2 commentary. **AD — surface adapter.** Conveys operations within one surface's verified affordances; holds every dated capability claim with its FR tag; can convey, format, and warn — never authorize; adapter capability never alters core semantics.

## 11. Intake and evolution operations

Intake pipeline: F01 (and F02 when activated) feed S01→S02→S03; interpretation (F03) and proposal (F04) are pull-based — triggered by H request, a flagged event, or a cadence parameter, never by autonomous initiative above the active automation stage. Evolution loop: experience → F12 postmortems → regression/improvement candidates → F04 decisions → F06 supersessions; project experience reaches global rules only through F10. All cadences (triage, digest, sweep, review batching) are explicit parameters; no cadence value is adopted here. Nothing in intake or evolution bypasses S06/S07; volume of intake never erodes the review gate (AUTO3 guard).

## 12. Handoff subsystem design

Built entirely on AC9 (A08); packages are immutable per issue, supersedable, never truth sources.
