## Decision in plain language

Decide whether to accept the Pro-corrected provisional baseline:

- keep the reusable capability catalogue in Mnemosyne for now;
- do not create a fourth shared repository;
- keep Meta-Agent methodology and target truth boundaries unchanged;
- keep target selection, adaptation and implementation target-local;
- prepare stable capability IDs, revisions and explicit split/merge/retire relationships as candidate mechanisms;
- use target-owned selection records and non-authoritative derived impact views;
- require a future explicit cutover decision before assigning catalogue ownership to Meta-Agent or a dedicated package.

## Options

### A — Accept modified provisional baseline (recommended)

Accept the direction as a candidate for bounded validation. This does not implement schema, migrate repositories or adopt it in targets.

### B — Accept Fable's stronger Meta-Agent ownership direction

Commission a separate cutover design deciding which capability families move to Meta-Agent. No migration occurs from this decision alone.

### C — Defer

Retain the report and adjudication as evidence, but wait for more target selections/real-use data before selecting a baseline.

### D — Reject

Reject role federation or the proposed lifecycle mechanism and state the preferred ownership model.

### E — Modify freely

The Owner may accept only selected parts, reject the premise, or provide another model.

## Consequences of deferral

No current truth changes. The capability catalogue remains a Mnemosyne candidate and later target work continues under existing boundaries, but version/impact ambiguity remains unresolved.
